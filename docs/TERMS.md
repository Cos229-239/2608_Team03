## 1. What Arv is

Arv records family members telling their stories, transcribes them on the device, and keeps
them in an archive on that device. It also holds documents, photographs, people, family
relationships, and health information a family chooses to write down.

Nothing in Arv is a backup service. See section 6.

## 4. Recording other people

This is the obligation that comes with an app like this.

An archive in Arv is mostly about people who are not you. Some of them are living and can
answer for themselves. Some of them are dead and cannot.

**You are responsible for having permission to record someone, and for the consequences of

The laws on recording another person's voice differ by state and country, and some places
require every person in a conversation to consent. Knowing which rules apply to you is your
responsibility, not ours.

If a person asks to be removed from an archive, honour it. Arv gives an owner and a keeper

## 6. There is no backup, and you can lose everything

Read this twice.

Arv keeps your archive on your phone and nowhere else. Nothing is uploaded. Nothing is
copied to a server. There is no account recovery for an archive, because there is nothing
held anywhere to recover it from.

That means an archive is lost, permanently and completely, if you:

- lose the phone, or it is stolen, or it breaks
- uninstall the app
- clear the app's data
- reset the phone or move to a new one

An archive can hold the only existing recording of someone's voice. **Use Settings to export
the archive to a zip file and keep that file somewhere else.** That export is the only copy
you will have. Doing it is your responsibility, and no part of this app will do it for you.

## 7. Health information

Arv can store health information a family writes down. It is a place to keep records, not a
medical tool.

Nothing in Arv is medical advice, a diagnosis, or a substitute for a clinician. The app
deliberately refuses to summarise or paraphrase health records for exactly this reason: three
relatives mentioning heart trouble is not a finding about your family, and reading records
belongs to a doctor.

A health record in Arv is controlled by the person it is about, or the memory steward they
named, rather than by whoever typed it in.
