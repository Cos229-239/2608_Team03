Arv Lo-Fi Wireframes

A private family network and living memory archive. Stay connected today, build the shared family story together, and preserve the voices of the people you are going to lose. Native Android, portrait, 360 × 800 dp frames. Grayscale on purpose: this pass is about structure, hierarchy, and flow, not color or brand.

22 screens. 01 to 16 are the month-one build. 17 to 22 cover the family network, the permission model, the librarians, and the Memory Garden that the roadmap ends in.

COS229 Project & Portfolio II · Milestone 01 · People rarely lose someone all at once. They lose the stories first.

Which teammate owns which screens

  • CAPTURE Audio and photo recording, media management, playback → 04, 05, 06, 07, 15
  • BACKEND Firebase, storage, sync, roles, the permission model → 02, 13, 14, 15, 16, 20
  • AI Transcription, search, prompts, the two librarians → 07, 08, 10, 12, 21
  • UI Timeline, feed, family tree, profiles, shell → 01, 03, 09, 11, 17, 18, 19
  • Phase 4, after this term: 22, the Memory Garden. Same archive, different door.
